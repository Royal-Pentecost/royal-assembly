Royal Membership app: privacy policy

How the Royal Membership app uses your information. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The Church of Pentecost, Royal Assembly (Ayigya District, Kumasi, Ghana) runs this app and is responsible for your information under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Questions go to the assembly office, below.

What we keep about you

Your name, phone number, date of birth, sex, marital status and where you live; your member ID, day-born group, ministries and any church office; your profile photo if one is added; and, from the church's membership form, your title, place of birth, hometown, GPS address, email, other phone numbers, next of kin, water and Holy Spirit baptism details, occupation, education, school or workplace, and whether you have a disability.

Attendance

Each time you mark attendance we record the service, the time, how it was marked (by you or by an administrator), and how far you were from the church. Attendance figures such as your Sunday streak are worked out from these records.

Location

The app reads your phone's location only while it is open, to show whether you are inside the church grounds. When you mark attendance your position is sent to the church server, which works out your distance from the church and keeps only that distance, not your position. Location is never collected in the background.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the sign-in code on the church website. No photo is taken or kept.

Your device

To send you notifications we keep your phone's notification token. We also note when the app last connected, to show whether a member is active.

Why we use it

To keep the church's membership register, record attendance, send church notices and reminders, carry the Bible study, and let you sign in to the app and the church website. We do not sell your information, show adverts, or use it for anything outside church administration.

Who can see it

You can see your own record. The assembly's administrators can see and correct members' records, including the membership form details. A day-born group's leader and executives can see the names and contact details of their own group. Church leaders' names, titles, photos and contact numbers are shown to members, including on the church website's members area.

Services that handle it for us

The church server and database are hosted by Render; photos are stored with Cloudinary; sign-in codes are sent by text through Moolre; notifications go through Google Firebase and Expo; the map comes from Google Maps; and the church website is hosted on GitHub Pages. They handle the information only to provide those services to the church.

How long we keep it

Your record and attendance history are kept while you are a member of the assembly. When you leave, or ask us to delete your account, your record, attendance and login are deleted within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have your account and data deleted. To delete, go to Settings, then Request account deletion, or contact the assembly office. You can turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings at any time; without location you cannot mark your own attendance, but an administrator can mark it for you.

Children

The app is meant for members of the church and is not directed at children. Children's details on the register are given by their parents or guardians and managed by the church's administrators.

Security

Information travels between the app and the server over encrypted connections. You sign in with a code sent to your phone, and logging out ends your session on that device. Only authorised administrators can change other members' records.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update it here and on the website. For any question or request, contact the Royal Assembly office on +233 53 709 6725.

Deleting your account

You can ask for your account and everything the app holds about you (your membership record, attendance history and login) to be deleted at any time.

  1. In the app, open Settings, then tap Request account deletion and confirm; or
  2. Contact the Royal Assembly office on +233 53 709 6725 and ask for your account to be deleted.

Your account and data are deleted within 30 days of the request. Nothing is kept afterwards except the church's overall attendance totals, which do not identify you.

Terms of use

By using this app you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy (Settings, Privacy & Security).

Who it is for

The app is for members and administrators of The Church of Pentecost, Royal Assembly. Your account is created by the church and tied to your phone number.

Using it fairly

Keep your phone and sign-in code to yourself. Mark attendance only for yourself, and only when you are at the service; administrators sign in members who cannot do it themselves.

Your record

Your membership record is kept by the assembly's administrators. If something is wrong, tell an administrator or the assembly office so it can be corrected.

Administrators

Administrators may see and correct members' records only for church administration, and every change they make is recorded.

Your information

Your information is used only for church administration, as the privacy policy explains, and is never sold.

Ending your account

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time from Settings. The church may also close an account that is no longer needed.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the app will show the new version. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
