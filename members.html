Getting started

  1. Install the app from the App Store or Google Play when it opens.
  2. Enter the phone number the church has for you.
  3. Type the code we send by SMS. You stay signed in for four weeks.

Number not working?

Your number must be in the church register. Speak to an usher or an admin on Sunday and they will add or correct it for you.
