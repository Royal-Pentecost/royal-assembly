In Ghana the day you were born gives you a name. Here it also gives you a Bible study class and a group of people who will know you by it.

Before your first visit

I do not know anyone. Is that okay?

Yes. Everyone was new once. Tell an usher at the door that it is your first time, and they will help you find a seat.

What time should I arrive?

Service starts at 9:00 AM every Sunday. Come ten minutes early so you can settle in before we begin.

Can I bring my children?

Please do. Children are part of our church family, and the Children's Ministry teaches them at their level.

How do I find the church?

Royal Assembly is in Ayigya, Kumasi. Tap Get directions, or search MCQH+MXJ Kumasi in Google Maps.
